# Governance of the ALMA public record

## Stewardship

Yernar Sailybayev is the initiator, original author and architect of ALMA and
maintains this public record. Later contributors are credited separately.

## Changes

Corrections and contributions may be proposed through a GitHub issue or pull
request. Acceptance does not transfer project authorship or create an
endorsement, partnership or right to use ALMA names for an independent service.

Changes to licensing scope, authorship, ALMA positioning, grant attribution or
approved public-media boundaries require explicit acceptance by the steward.

## Protected boundaries

This repository must not receive field evidence, photographs, exact sensitive
coordinates, personal data, credentials, private GIS packages, closed legal
review records or production configuration.

Legal Core decisions are not created or changed through this public record.
Legal references used by an ALMA deployment must come from controlled cards
reviewed by an authorised legal reviewer and accepted through the deployment's
administrative process.

## Names and releases

Official ALMA releases are identified by the steward, a version, a date and a
verifiable commit or checksum. Independent derivatives must follow
`TRADEMARKS.md` and must not imply official status.
