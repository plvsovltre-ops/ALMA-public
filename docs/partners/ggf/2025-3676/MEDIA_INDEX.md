# Public media index

## Approved public references

1. **ALMA public site** — <https://www.alma.eco>

   Public history, methodology, aggregated map and project explanation. Exact
   active-case coordinates and personal data are not published.

2. **Mergin Maps case study** —
   <https://merginmaps.com/case-studies/protecting-historic-almaty-orchards-with-mergin-maps-and-gemini>

   An independent published account of the early field-data work. A later
   update may describe the controlled evidence and Legal Core architecture.

3. **This GGF public record**

   Publication-safe outcomes, relationship to OASIS, licensing and attribution.

4. **ALMA Monitor public code and operational status** —
   <https://github.com/plvsovltre-ops/alma-monitor>

   Public v1 source, tests, governance artifacts and a dated statement that
   separates the launched closed pilot from the private Monitor 2 successor.

5. **ALMA and comparable systems**

   A publication-safe Russian-language comparison of ALMA with selected citizen
   science, civic technology and Rules as Code systems. It documents the scope
   and limits of the comparison and does not claim endorsement or patent
   novelty.

## Materials requiring separate clearance

- field photographs and full observation dossiers;
- images containing faces, vehicle plates, homes or sensitive locations;
- screenshots showing private project URLs, members, email addresses or access
  controls;
- exact active-case coordinates;
- original archival pages whose reuse terms have not been confirmed;
- financial records, receipts and correspondence.

GGF may redistribute only the public materials identified above or a later asset
explicitly cleared in writing. The open-source licence does not override privacy,
safeguarding or third-party rights.
