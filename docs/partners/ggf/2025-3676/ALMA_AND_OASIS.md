# ALMA and OASIS

ALMA is the working digital and evidence infrastructure. It connects a citizen
observation with photographs, coordinates, time, reviewed geographic context,
controlled statements about facts, human-approved legal cards and a draft
request asking a competent institution to verify the circumstances.

OASIS is the broader citizen-science system in which ALMA is being integrated.
Its mission extends beyond one application or one landscape. OASIS provides the
institutional and programme environment for research questions, volunteer
protocols, learning, data quality, thematic programmes, community participation
and cooperation with professional and institutional partners.

This relationship is deliberate:

- ALMA makes field participation operational, traceable and reproducible;
- OASIS connects that capability to a wider public-interest mission and a
  sustainable institutional setting;
- ALMA remains a portable system that may be deployed outside OASIS;
- OASIS does not replace the authorship, ownership or identity of ALMA.

The GGF grant supported the initial development of ALMA, not the full creation
or continuing operation of OASIS. Its lasting value is that it enabled the
practical core around which a broader citizen-science system could be built.

A future relationship with GGF should be described as a separate possibility,
not as an existing commitment: a limited, measurable pilot could test whether
the ALMA field-evidence workflow is useful to grassroots grantees within the
OASIS programme environment.
